# CC0 AI Asset Pack

Normally, I hate AI art. Despite that, I do sometimes experiment with it out of curiosity. A lot of the time it mutates things, produces weird artifacts, and generally looks weird as shit.

I was bored one day and started experimenting with map-making textures. If I could actually knit or embroider these myself, I would have made them by hand and taken photographs. Until I can do that, I made AI-generated versions instead.

These particular experiments came out pretty, so I'm releasing them here under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

EDIT:
added some stained glass experiments to the collection.


Why CC0?

1. An AI generated the base images.
2. It doesn't feel right to claim exclusive ownership over artwork I didn't physically create myself.

So: if you want to use them, use them. If you don't, don't.

Please don't yell at me for generating images with AI. I'm being completely open about what they are, how they were made, and why I'm releasing them this way.

## AI Generation Disclosure

The base images in this collection were generated using OpenAI image-generation tools and curated and/or edited by the collection creator. No third-party artwork was intentionally used as source material.
